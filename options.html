Wikidata Source Saver

Copy a Wikidata item ID (e.g. Q42 or its URL), go to a configured website, and press the shortcut (or click the toolbar icon). It adds P1343: <website item> with qualifier P2699: <page URL> to the item. Change the shortcut at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
